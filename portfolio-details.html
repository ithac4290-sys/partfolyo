Lumen Analytics — a calmer way to read your data

Lumen needed a dashboard that turned a firehose of metrics into something a busy founder could scan in seconds. We rebuilt the information architecture from the ground up and shipped a focused, accessible interface.

User testing revealed that decluttering non-essential metrics and highlighting focal graphs accelerated executive decision-making speed by 40%.

A modular, scalable design system allowed engineering to ship subsequent analytical views twice as fast.
